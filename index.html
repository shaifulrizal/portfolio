Shaiful RizalCreative leadership · Visual communication

Good design helps people understand. Great creative direction brings the right people and ideas together to make that happen.

My work connects visual storytelling, multimedia production and digital experiences. At Tenaga Nasional Berhad, I lead creative production and collaborate with stakeholders to turn complex messages into communication that people can connect with.

I care about the thinking as much as the final visual: who we are speaking to, what matters to them, and what they should take away.
